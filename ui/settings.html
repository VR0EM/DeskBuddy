About DeskBuddy

What you type stays yours

DeskBuddy only counts how many keys and clicks you make, and whether a key is on the left or the right of your keyboard (so it knows which paw to use). Which key you press is never looked at or stored, and DeskBuddy never connects to the internet.

Your settings, buddies and counters live in a folder on your own computer.

Handy tricks

  • Drag to move your buddy. It remembers where it sits.
  • Clicking your buddy makes it happy.
  • Double-click opens this window, right-click gives a quick menu.
  • In the way? Turn on Ghost mode and click right through it.
  • After a while without input it falls asleep. One key and it's awake again.

Light on your computer

No Steam and no heavy programs in the background. DeskBuddy uses the web view that's already part of your system, and only redraws when something changes.
